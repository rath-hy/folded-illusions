# Flower cut-outs for the backdrop

Cut out of three photos from Wikimedia Commons, saved as fresh transparent PNGs (no metadata), and resized. [`layout.json`](layout.json) places them on the backdrop; [`../../obama_backdrop.ipynb`](../../obama_backdrop.ipynb) uses both.

| Files | Source | Author | License |
|---|---|---|---|
| `chrysanthemum_1.png` – `chrysanthemum_3.png` (single blooms) | [Chrysanthemum × Hortorum Multiflora](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:2026.10.17_Chrysanthemum_%C3%97_Hortorum_Multiflora.jpg) | Agbarto | CC0 |
| `jasmine_1.png` – `jasmine_3.png` (round patches of the hedge) | [Gelsomino - Jasminum polyanthum - Flowers and buds](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Gelsomino_-_Jasminum_polyanthum_-_Flowers_and_buds.jpg) | Anna.Massini | [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) |
| `agapanthus_1.png` (the umbel) | [Agapanthus Blue Giant](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Agapanthus_Blue_Giant.jpg) | Pamela Carls | [CC BY 2.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/2.0/) |

Changes: each flower was cut out by its color (chrysanthemums and agapanthus) or as a soft round patch (jasmine), cropped, and resized to at most 900 pixels.
